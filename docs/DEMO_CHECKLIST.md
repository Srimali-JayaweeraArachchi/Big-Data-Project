# 5-10 Minute Demo Checklist

1. Show `docker compose ps` with healthy core services.
2. Show smart-meter JSON events in producer logs.
3. Show Spark `micro_batch_completed` structured logs.
4. Open the dashboard and explain zone load, solar, and renewable ratio.
5. Show a low-renewable or high-load alert.
6. Open Airflow and show the successful tariff DAG.
7. Show the consolidated billing table.
8. Open `/health` and `/metrics`.
9. Briefly stop the producer and explain the no-data rule (a screenshot of the unhealthy state saves demo time).
10. Close with Kappa justification, limitations, and production improvements.
